      INDIAN FAMILY KITCHEN RULES & BUDGET

      • 1. The Cooking Oil Trap: 1 extra tbsp oil = 120 kcal. Ask your family to scoop your dal/sabzi before the final heavy ghee/oil tadka. Cook your eggs and chicken with 1 measured teaspoon (5 g) oil (~20 to 25 g oil/day total).
      • 2. Standard Indian Conversions: 1 medium phulka/roti (no ghee) = 30 g dry atta (102 kcal, 3.6g P, 21g C). 150 g cooked rice = 50 g raw rice (195 kcal). Dal/rajma/chole weights are dry weight (30 g dry ≈ 1 thick katori).
      • 3. Homemade Hung Curd: Strain toned curd in a muslin cloth in the fridge for 3 to 4 hours → 8 g protein / 100 g at one-third the cost of commercial Greek yogurt.
      • 4. Monthly Budget (₹7,750 to 10,150): 180 eggs (~₹1.2k), 6.5 kg chicken breast (~₹1.8k), 21 L toned milk/curd (~₹1.3k), soya/dal/oats/paneer (~₹1.3k), whey + creatine (~₹2k). Drop whey and use soya/egg whites to run at ~₹6,000/mo.
